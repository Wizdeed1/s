                How to use

                Every month

                1. In Genesys Cloud, export the month's Monthly Adherence, Agent Performance Summary, Schedule (activities) and Agent Status Timeline Summary.
                2. Drop all four on Import, with every supervisor's attendance sheet. Each card shows what was found. The bell at the top lists anything the tool fixed, anything you need to decide (for example two files that disagree on a day), and differences between the attendance and Genesys.
                3. On Review, open Issues: link each unknown name to a person as their system name, or add them. When a roster name is clearly one Genesys name, the tool links it by itself (the bell shows it, with Undo). Set people who left to Inactive in People.
                4. People shows a card per agent with their Genesys name, National ID, brand, team and this month's numbers. Point at any agent, anywhere in the tool, for a quick profile; select the card for the full page, where you can edit their details, see their history chart and export it. Switch to Table to edit many people at once.
                5. Check Preview, then press Build Excel.
                6. Performance (optional): export the Agent Performance Summary a second time with every column (Answered, Handle, Avg Handle, Avg Talk, Avg Hold, Avg ACW, Held, Transferred, Outbound) and drop it on Import. Preview then shows each employee's performance with team totals and a sortable table (green at least 15% better than the team, red at least 15% worse), and each person's page compares them with their team. Times are weighted the way Genesys works them out. Exports of past months go to the history, so the person's chart can show answered, handle time, hold, ACW and transfer rate by month. An export with only voice also gives the AHT.
                7. Workforce and exceptions: with the Schedule and Agent Status Timeline in, every scheduled day of the month is judged with the same rules as the Daily WFM & Performance Review (late after 0 min, no sign-on after 180 min, short of shift from 15 min, adherence 95%, AHT ceiling 2:30, high hold 45 s, at least 5 calls; leave days left out) and added up per employee: shifts, worked, no sign-on, late sign-ons and minutes, short of shift, break changes, time signed on, occupancy, conformance and exceptions. Preview shows the totals by team, the priority follow-up list with reasons and a sortable table; each person's page lists their days one by one. Late first call, calls under 10 s, longest calls and dropped calls need the daily Interactions export, so they stay in the daily email.
                8. Build the incentive data workbook too. Both files are filled in, including attendance and tardy minutes.
                9. On Email, pick Deduction or Incentive, then For review. Use Copy for Outlook and paste into a new email, or Download .eml and open it in Outlook. The data sent for review is remembered. The WFM manager and the senior manager send the deduction and absences emails from WFM › Emails, from the shared month (with the supervisors' attendance corrections).
                10. After the review deadline, drop any corrected files, open Final, and send. The final email lists what changed since the review.
                11. The daily emails are built into the tool. A WFM analyst drops the day's exports on WFM › Report builder (on a Sunday, Thursday to Saturday): MC Leaders and the WFM Daily Review are made and sent from there, and Virgin Postpaid, Virgin Mobile, FRiENDi Mobile and FRiENDi Pay appear straight away on the WFM manager's WFM › Daily emails, where he writes the SLA insight, checks them and sends them (Copy for Outlook or Open in Outlook), then marks each one sent. The emails are made by the same parts as Member Care Report Builder 14, so they look exactly as before. Every day dropped is kept for everyone; building the WFM Daily Review also keeps each agent's day, and a month without monthly exports is shown from the stored days.
                12. Supervisors: drop the Teams Distribution email (.msg) on Import, or paste its table with Review › People › Supervisors…. The tool shows who changes team before anything is saved; names it cannot match are listed so you can fix them in the roster's Supervisor column.
                13. Saving: while you build a month, its results are saved to the shared history by themselves, a few seconds after each change (the line at the top says when). A month already in the history from an older file is kept until you replace it in Review › History, and a month the senior manager has finalized is never overwritten.
                14. For a one-to-one, open a person and use One-to-one form, then Final form after the investigation. Both download as PDF.
                15. KPI results: drop the monthly KPI file (DRC-CSAT, Quality, Empowerment, FCR…) on Import. Adherence, AHT, days under target and tardiness come from the exports and the attendance. Review › KPI results shows every agent's ranking, final %, incentive target and earned SR, with PDF cards, Excel and a copy-for-email card. Scorecards are in Settings.
                16. History: drop the past monthly incentive files (MC_all_team_incentive _data) and the Genesys exports of past months (AHT, Agent Performance, Monthly Adherence, Schedule, Agent Status Timeline) on Import or in Review › History. Each file goes to its own month; a month still running is left out. The incentive file's values are kept, and the Genesys exports fill the gaps and add deduction minutes and conformance (only for agents whose schedule covers the whole month, so drop all the schedule files that touch a month together). Each person's page shows their months as a chart, and Review › History shows everyone side by side. The month you send as final is added by itself.

                Rules used

                1. Adherence: a blank day, or one under the NA cut-off, is NA. Avr. is the average of the other days.
                2. Deduction: Sched. is the minutes of On Queue, Break, Meal, Update Break Time and Training. Actual is the time in On Queue, Break, Meal, Training or Meeting during those segments. Diff is Sched. minus Actual, never below 0, and 0 when Actual is 0.
                3. Inactive people with fewer attended days than the limit are left out of every report. They stay in the Roster and are listed in the Overview.
                4. Names: the system name is used when filled, otherwise the name. Spaces and capital letters don't matter.
                5. Attendance: codes like "Off", "AL leave" or "SL leaves" are tidied to OFF, AL and SL. A number in a day cell is tardy minutes; when the day cells and a Tardy column disagree, the day cells are used until you choose. Each fix can be undone from the bell.

                Roles and pages

                • Supervisor › Team: their own agents (the roster's Supervisor column), the team's average of every KPI (their own KPIs as a supervisor), their supervisor scorecard, a one-to-one board, the month's attendance (counted from Genesys; only the supervisor can correct a day), the agents waiting for their approval, and Finish my team, which tells the senior manager in the tool and makes an email draft to him. It stays grey until every agent's one-to-one is done. The one-to-one: arrange it; an agent with an account fills in their part on their own page (what was discussed, the root cause, their comments, actions, the next review date) and sends it; the supervisor reads it, accepts or rejects it with a final comment and sends the final back; only then does the agent sign. For an agent without an account the supervisor fills in the form when they meet. Supervisors do not see the incentive amounts.
                • Senior manager › Supervisors: the one-to-one deadline, which supervisors have finished (a notification and a count on the page button when one does), the supervisors' scorecards and their items (team averages, one-to-ones by the deadline, the quality team's leader items, or items he types), and the month's four emails: one-to-ones ready (to the supervisors), the deadline reminder (to those not finished), the results for review (“Hi All”: the ID, name, final % and earned SR of everyone on the incentive list who earned it, with the same table as an Excel file) and the final result form (his own form: everyone on the incentive list with their final % and earned SR, the month's total and the table the VP, HR Director, Finance Director and CEO sign, as an email and as the same Excel form). The incentive list on the same page says who is on those two, in order, with the names as the tool has them; add or remove people there. The emails carry no logo or name: Outlook adds your signature. Results: every agent; he decides who is eligible against the rules and finalizes the month.
                • WFM: Analytics by team, brand and month; Agents with every measure and the best and worst quarter marked; Daily with the stored days; Report builder (the WFM analysts: the day's exports, MC Leaders, the WFM Daily Review); Daily emails (the WFM manager: the four brand emails to send); Interactions from the interactions export; Emails with the deduction and absences emails (the WFM manager). The senior manager and the analysts read the rest of the page.
                • Team page for the senior manager, the WFM team and the admin: every team; the agents ranked lowest first (adherence, final %, exceptions, absence or tardiness), with a name search and the supervisor under each name.
                • Quality team › Quality: drop the Final EVAL; the final score fills Quality Observations, critical errors are kept (an attitude error stops the incentive), the Leaders sheet scores the team leads. Any quality KPI can be corrected by hand until the month is final; each change is logged.
                • Agent › My page: only their own results, attendance, daily adherence and forms. They fill in their part of the one-to-one and send it to their supervisor; when the supervisor sends the final back, they read it and sign it on screen, download the signed PDF and email it to themselves and their supervisor.
                • WFM analyst and admin build the month. Incentive: base by brand (Virgin 1000, FRiENDi 500, FRiENDi Pay 500, a person's own base in the roster wins); nobody earns it with a final under 80%, an attitude critical error or fewer than 5 days attended, unless the senior manager decides otherwise.

                Closing a month

                Supervisors › Month close shows who does what by which day of the next month: the month built from the exports (day 2), the KPI file and the EVAL (day 3), the results on the agents' pages and the ready email (day 5), the objections answered (day 8), the one-to-ones and the review (day 9), the final and the final emails (day 10). Most steps tick themselves when the tool sees them done; tick the others when they are done outside the tool. Late steps are red, the next two days amber. The senior manager can change the due days under Due days.

                Today, at the top of each person's first page, lists what is theirs to do now: their late month-close steps, one-to-ones to hold, objections to answer or fix, agents waiting for approval, forms waiting for a signature, last month's actions to check, a daily review not stored yet, names to link, accounts waiting. Each line opens the place to do it; Hide for today folds it to one line.

                Objections

                An agent who thinks a number is wrong raises an objection on their own page: a day of attendance or adherence (select the day), a KPI, the AHT, the quality result, the incentive, or anything else, with what it should be and why. The window closes on the month's objection day. The supervisor accepts it (attendance is corrected straight away), says why not, or sends it to the senior manager; an agent whose objection was not accepted can ask the senior manager to look, once. An accepted objection that the WFM team, the quality team or the senior manager must correct waits on their page until they mark it fixed. The agent sees every answer. Supervisors › Objections shows each team's dispute rate.

                Coaching that follows up

                A one-to-one starts with what was agreed last month: each action (Done, Partly, Not done) and each KPI then and now (better or worse). The PDF and the agent's copy carry it, and the board shows "Follow-up 2/3". The senior manager can count it in the supervisors' scorecard: add an item from Follow-up of last month's one-to-ones (actions done, or KPIs better).

                This month so far (agents)

                While a month runs, each stored daily review updates every agent's page: the projected final % and incentive if the month stays like this, and the weakest KPI to work on first. The KPIs that come only at the end of the month use last month's values until this month's arrive. Agents can read their page in Arabic (العربية at the top of their page).

                Incentive rules

                The scorecards (KPIs, weights, bands), the incentive bases and who earns the incentive (lowest final %, fewest days attended) are one set of rules. Only the senior manager or an admin changes them (Supervisors › Incentive rules, or Settings › Scorecards); everyone else sees them read-only. Start new rules from a month: that month and the ones after it use the new set, the months before keep theirs, and a final month never changes. Every change is logged. Supervisors › Changes by hand lists, for a month, everything changed by hand: eligibility and results, attendance days, quality numbers, objections, the rules, one-to-ones changed after the agent signed. It downloads as Excel.

                Parallel run and the EVAL with IDs

                While the team still works the old way, drop the old Excel of the month on Results (the final result with ID, Name, Final % and Earned SR, or the incentive data workbook): the tool lines every person up by national ID, then by name, and lists what differs with the likely reason. The quality team can download EVAL template with IDs on the Quality page: an EVAL workbook with every agent's national ID, so a filled-in EVAL links every row by its ID.

                Search

                Press Ctrl K (or /) on any page, or click Search, and type in plain words: a name, a measure, a month or a day, a team, a brand, a supervisor or a page. For example Tala AHT, lowest adherence Virgin, adherence below 95%, absent yesterday, below target August, supervisors not finished, one-to-ones not held, my one-to-one, or a how-to question. The answer shows straight away; Tab completes the word being typed, and the buttons under the box add the likely next word. Typos and a few Arabic words are understood.

                The search finds only what your role may see: a supervisor their own team, the quality team the KPI results, an agent their own page. It remembers what you pick, in this browser only, to suggest it first; Clear my searches forgets it.

                The shared folder

                1. Put this HTML in the common folder, with an empty folder next to it, for example MC Data.
                2. The first person opens the HTML in Edge or Chrome, chooses the data folder, and makes the admin account. Everyone else opens the same HTML, chooses the same folder once (the browser remembers it), and makes an account with a username.
                3. The admin gives staff their role in Manage accounts, links supervisors to their name in the roster (only the operations team is listed) and ticks the agents each supervisor leads. Agents choose their supervisor when they sign up; the supervisor approves them.

                The data files are locked: staff accounts open them with the team's key, an agent's account only the agent's own place, both unlocked by the person's password. Opened outside the tool, a file is unreadable. Anyone who can delete files in the common folder can still delete them, so keep the folder's permissions to the people who need it, and keep it in the usual IT backup. The tool picks up other people's changes every few seconds.

                Accounts

                Everyone signs in with a username and a password; the sign-up asks no email. The first account becomes the admin. A user who forgets their password asks the admin, who resets it; in the shared folder their access is then opened again by the admin (or, for an agent, by their supervisor).

                Your data

                Export files are read on your computer and never uploaded. What is kept: the roster, the months' results (one row per agent), the quality numbers, the forms and signatures, and day totals of the daily data and of the interactions export (never the callers' numbers). With the shared folder it is kept there, locked; otherwise in this browser on this computer. Use Export roster for a backup copy of the roster.
