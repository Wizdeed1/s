Video Splitter

Cut a long video into parts of up to 30 MB, named 001.mp4, 002.mp4 and so on. Then save them with a video-notes.md file and frame images that Claude can read. Everything runs in your browser. The video is not uploaded anywhere.

2 · Settings

MB here means 1,000,000 bytes, so every part is under 30 MB whichever unit a tool uses. The tool first measures the video, then fills each part as close to the limit as it can, so you get as few parts as possible. Parts are copied, not re-encoded, so quality is unchanged. Large files are fine: the video is read from disk in pieces. MP4, MOV, M4V and MKV files work best.

3 · Split

In Chrome and Edge you first choose where to save. A new folder is made there and each part is written into it as soon as it is cut. Other browsers download the files at the end instead.

4 · Parts and notes

Optional. Write what happens in each part, or paste its transcript or subtitles. The notes go into video-notes.md, which Claude can read next to the parts and the frame images.
