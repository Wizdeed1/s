Video Splitter

Cut a long video into parts of up to 30 MB, named 001.mp4, 002.mp4 and so on. Then save them with a video-notes.md file and frame images that Claude can read. Everything runs in your browser. The video is not uploaded anywhere.

2 · Settings

MB here means 1,000,000 bytes, so every part is under 30 MB whichever unit a tool uses. Parts are copied, not re-encoded, so they are fast and keep the original quality. A cut can only happen at a keyframe, so a part may come out a little bigger than planned. If that happens the tool cuts again with a smaller target. MP4, MOV and M4V files work best.

4 · Parts and notes

Optional. Write what happens in each part, or paste its transcript or subtitles. The notes go into video-notes.md, which Claude can read next to the parts and the frame images.
